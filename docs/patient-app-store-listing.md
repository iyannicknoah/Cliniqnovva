## Full description

Cliniqnovva Patient is the easiest way to manage your care at clinics
across Rwanda that use Cliniqnovva.

- **Book appointments** at any participating clinic — pick a department,
  service, and doctor, then choose a time that works for you.
- **Message your clinic** directly and hear back from the care team.
- **View your medical records and receipts** from every visit, in one
  place, no matter which Cliniqnovva clinic you visited.
- **Leave a review** after a visit and see how the clinic responds.
- **Get reminders** before your appointments so you never miss one.

Already a patient at a Cliniqnovva clinic? Register with the same phone
number you gave at the front desk and we'll offer to link your existing
records — no need to start over.

Available in Kinyarwanda, English, and French.
